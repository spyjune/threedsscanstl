How to scan

  1. Place the object on a stable surface.
  2. Use bright, even lighting.
  3. Keep the object inside the square.
  4. Move slowly around the object.
  5. Capture overlapping views.
  6. Capture the top and all sides.

Your iPhone 15 camera is used for the scan. The captured images can be processed into a 3D mesh and eventually exported as an STL.
